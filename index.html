About

Every piece starts as a drawing.

MONTA is a one-person studio. I sketch every print and pattern by hand, then work with a small family-run workshop to produce each design in a single run of around fifty pieces.

No seasons on repeat, no overstock, no discount bins. When a run sells out, it's gone — and the next drawing goes to print.

  • 50pieces per run
  • 100%organic cotton
  • 1workshop, 12 people

Questions

How long does shipping take?

Orders are packed within 2 working days. UK delivery takes 2–4 days; international 5–10 days. You'll get a tracking link by email.

Can I return or exchange?

Yes — within 30 days of delivery, unworn and with tags. Email us with your order number and we'll send a returns label.

How do the sizes fit?

Tops are cut boxy and relaxed. If you prefer a closer fit, size down. Every product page has a size guide with garment measurements.

How should I care for my pieces?

Wash cold, inside out, and dry flat. Skip the tumble dryer and your prints will last for years.

Will a sold-out design come back?

Rarely. Each run is a one-off, but now and then a favourite gets a second run — follow us on Instagram to hear first.
